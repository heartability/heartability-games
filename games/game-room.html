Dear visitor,

Welcome to Heartability. I have played this game in my journals for years. It helped me make so many of my dreams come true. Now I offer it to you. I believe this way of approaching life has the power to change the world, to create a world where people trust their intuition and following your own path is considered normal, not alternative.

The games on this page take inspiration from the basic settings controls of your typical video game, but instead apply that world building technique to your personal life. Build a map to explore your emotional landscape. Play bingo to check in on your energy reserves. Stare into the infinity mirror and imagine what version of yourself you want to be next.

Visit occasionally, or enter the matrix and start exploring your future with Heartability. There's no wrong way to play. Nothing to optimize. Just endless possibilities for you to explore.

I believe in you,

Zoe Tinnes, founder of Heartability
