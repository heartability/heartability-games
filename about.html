Heartability is a self care game designed to help you gain perspective over your life trajectory — not what an algorithm thinks you want, not what a wellness influencer is selling. Just you, your imagination, and the magic of showing up for yourself.

The four core games — Treasure Map, What's in your Bag, Self Care Bingo, and Infinity Mirror — take inspiration from the basic settings controls of your typical video game, but instead apply that world building technique to your personal life. Build a map to explore your emotional landscape. Fill your inventory to assess your current routines. Play bingo to check in on energy reserves. Stare into the infinity mirror and imagine what version of yourself you want to be next. There's no wrong way to play, and nothing to optimize. Just endless possibilities.

We built this because the creative process deserves better tools. The world is full of recommendations and routines that were made for someone else. Heartability was made from the lived experience of forging a new path and having to figure it out alone. Now you can figure it out with Heartability.

Enter the castle, the door is open.

— with love, Zoe Tinnes, founder of Heartability
